Our Story & Mission

About Us & Background

Startup background, mission, vision, objectives, and team members behind WellNest.

The Problem

Many students and busy people forget appointments, medications, and daily wellness tasks. Health records and reliable health information are hard to organize and access when needed most.

The WellNest Solution

WellNest provides a simple digital platform for organizing health activities, receiving reliable reminders, and learning from certified health sources in one intuitive sanctuary.

M

Our Mission

To empower students and communities with accessible, streamlined health routine management aligned with global wellness standards.

V

Our Vision

To become the leading regional digital health companion fostering proactive wellness habits and stress-free routine tracking.

O

Key Objectives

Deliver intuitive reminder tools, reliable educational hubs, and school wellness campaign partnerships across Panabo City.
